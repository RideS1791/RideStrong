# RideStrong

Strength, core and mobility training built around dumbbells and bodyweight. Runs on your phone like an app, works offline, and keeps everything on your phone.

## Install on iPhone
1. Open https://rides1791.github.io/RideStrong/ in **Safari**.
2. Tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.

## What it does
- **12 week plan** in four blocks: Foundation, Build, Strength and power, then Maintain. Sets and reps step up automatically as the weeks go on.
- **Your level**: beginner, intermediate or advanced. Each level gets different sets, reps and easier or harder versions of the exercises.
- **Your week**: choose your strength days (1 to 6).
- **Workouts by goal**: Leg Strength, Legs and Core, Power (no jumping), Core Strength, Six Pack Abs, Obliques, Quick Core, Lean Upper Body, Upper Body and Core, Posture, Full Body, Fat Burn Circuit, No Equipment, Mobility, Stretch. Plus your own workouts.
- **Variety**: every workout rotates through versions A, B and C, and leg sessions finish with a core block.
- **Any workout, any day**: whatever you choose counts towards that week's plan.
- **3D animated demonstrations** for every exercise in the plans (from the free ExerciseDB V1 service by AscendAPI), with photos and step by step instructions as well. 876 exercises in the full library.
- **Guided mode** like BetterMe: a countdown clock on every exercise, the how to steps on screen, one exercise at a time, full screen animation, weight and rep buttons, countdowns for timed moves, rest screen with what's up next, and a voice coach.
- **Live workout**: warm up, then each set logged as weight and reps, reps only, or time, with what you did last time, an automatic rest timer and countdowns for timed holds.
- **Progress**: strength sessions and rides per week, personal bests and full history.

## Your data
Everything is stored in Safari on your phone only. Use **Settings, Export backup** now and then, and **Restore from backup** on a new phone.

## Credits
Exercise photos and instructions are from [Free Exercise DB](https://github.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db), released into the public domain. Exercise animations are loaded from ExerciseDB V1 (AscendAPI, static.exercisedb.dev), free tier, for personal use.

This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice. Stop any exercise that causes pain and get advice from a GP or physiotherapist if you have an injury or health condition.

## Daily life features
- One minute quiz on first open: goal, body, activity, focus areas, level, equipment, days. Builds the plan and daily targets.
- Your day on the Today screen: water glasses, steps, and optional meal ideas, breathing, mood and a daily tip (switch each on or off in Settings).
- Challenges: 14, 21 and 28 day programmes with a day calendar. Workouts started from a challenge tick their day off automatically.
- Body: weight chart with goal line, measurements, progress photos (stored on the phone only), BMI.
- Badges for workouts, streaks, challenges, water, steps and weigh ins.
