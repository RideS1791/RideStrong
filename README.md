# RideStrong

A simple workout app for your phone. Pick a workout, see how every exercise is done, tick off your sets and keep a log of your progress. No account, no subscription, no adverts.

## Install on iPhone
1. Open the GitHub Pages address for this repository in **Safari** on your iPhone.
2. Tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
3. Open RideStrong from your home screen. It works offline once it has loaded.

## What it does
- **Today**: a suggested workout, your day streak, this week's workouts and your last session.
- **Workouts**: seven ready made workouts (full body at home, full body with dumbbells, legs and glutes, upper body, core, glutes at home, stretch and mobility), plus your own workouts that you build from any exercise.
- **Exercises**: a library of 876 exercises you can search and filter by muscle and equipment. Each one has start and finish photos that play like an animation, step by step instructions and your personal best.
- **Live workout**: log weight and reps, reps only, or time for each set. It shows what you did last time, has a rest timer after each set and a countdown for timed holds such as planks.
- **Progress**: workouts per week, personal bests per exercise and your full history.
- **Settings**: kg or lb, rest timer length, sound, and backup and restore.

## Your data
Everything is stored in Safari on your phone only. Nothing is sent anywhere. Use **Settings, Export backup** now and then, and **Restore from backup** on a new phone. History from the earlier version of RideStrong is brought across automatically the first time the new version opens.

## Exercise photos and instructions
From [Free Exercise DB](https://github.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db), released into the public domain. Photos for the ready made workouts are stored in this repository so they work offline. Photos for other exercises load from Free Exercise DB the first time you view them and are then kept on your phone.
